## Tata Elxsi: Senior Embedded Software Engineer

Philadelphia, US · November 2018 - Present · Project client: **Comcast**

### OTT apps for RDK (C/C++)

Led design and development of the premium-app porting layer for Amazon Prime, HBO Max, YouTube and Netflix in the RDK community.

- Developed and maintained the Netflix integration and device porting layer, following the common port architecture and RDK standards.
- Deep knowledge of the Netflix integration stack: player/GStreamer, DRM, authentication and authorization, and application-platform interfaces.
- Owned end-to-end Netflix certification: test execution, test-case analysis, debugging, fixes, validation, and coordination with SoC teams.
- Worked extensively with the Netflix Testing Suite (NTS) and EyePatch analysis to find and debug failures across application and platform layers.
- Integrated and validated new Netflix features, including Cloud Gaming and Multiplayer.
- Developed and maintained the Netflix launcher integration on RDK platforms.
- Working knowledge of Amazon Prime Video and YouTube integration requirements in RDK.

### Reference Home UI for RDK video devices (Lightning/JavaScript)

- Redesigned the JavaScript/Lightning RDK UI/UX to support the DAC (Downloadable Application Container) architecture.
- Oversaw UI implementation, integration and validation against DAC and RDK platform requirements.
- Strong understanding of application lifecycle through the Home UI: discovery, launch and management.
- Hands-on with DIAL (Discovery and Launch) and Device Automation Bus (DAB) integration with the Home UI.

### Provisioning & cryptography for native apps (C/C++)

- Strong grounding in cryptographic APIs used for OTT authentication and provisioning: encryption, HMAC, Diffie-Hellman key exchange.
- Contributed to the iCrypto Thunder plugin.
- Built a cryptography abstraction layer supporting multiple providers: OpenSSL, SecAPI and direct SoC crypto calls.
- Supported integration of SoC Trusted Application (TA) libraries, primarily for Netflix.

### DRM, OCDM & Thunder plugins (C/C++)

- Strong understanding of DRM and content protection: OCDM, Widevine, PlayReady and Secure Video Path (SVP) within RDK.
- Hands-on DRM integration and troubleshooting across application, OCDM and SoC layers.
- Developed Thunder plugins for RDK, including the Continue Watching plugin.
